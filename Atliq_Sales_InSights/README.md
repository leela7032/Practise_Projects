## Problem Statement
So we have a company called **ATLIQ Hardware Manufacturer** so **Sales Director Bhavin Patel** is facing some issues in terms of sales and so they want to hire Data Analyst / Business Analyst to solve this problem.

##Solution
First i have analyzed the problem and come up with AMES Grid (4 Steps)
 -- 1)**What is the Purpose ? **So here to unlock the sales insights that are not visible before for sales team for decision support and automate them to reduced manual time spent in data gathering.
 -- 2)**Who are the Stakeholders for the soluton?** So We considered for this project (Sales Director, Marketing Team, Customer Service Team ,Data &Analytics team and IT Software team)
 -- 3)**What is the END Result ?** So we decided to build an automated dashboard providing quick and latest sales insights in order to support data driven decision making .
 -- 4)**Success Criteria** So now Analysts don't need to gather information manually for investing the amount and this saves **70% ** manual time for decision making according to report.

 ##Sales Insights
 --1) What is the Total Revenue of the Company?
 --2)What are the total sales quantity up to date?
 --3)What is the Revenue per Market / Area?
 --4)What are the Sales Quantity per Market?
 --5)Top 5 customers according to Revenue?
 --6)Top 5 product by Revenue?
 --7) What is the Revenue Trend according to Quarter of year?
 --8) We have slicers that can help with particular Year and quarter?
